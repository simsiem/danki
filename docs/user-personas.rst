Motivation of Danki
===================

Make it easy for students to learn foreign languages and reduce time spent on vocabulary learning for school.


User segments
=============

Danki targets school students learning their first or second foreign language.


User persona: Student
=====================

Profile
-------

Jobs
''''

* Learn and retain vocabulary for school
* Work on texts as homework
* Prepare for tests
* Follow a homework-driven workflow: new vocabulary, text homework, and tests according to the teacher's schedule.
  
Pains
'''''

* Overwhelming amount of items in existing vocabulary apps (like Phase 6)
* Learning schema of existing vocabulary apps disconnected from teacher-assigned homework
* Mixing up similar headwords
* Text work is tedious because the student cannot remember many words in the text and lookups slow progress

Gains
'''''

* Teacher appreciates that the student knows certain words at school
* Good marks at school
* Faster text homework
* High success rate in reviews of vocabulary entries in the app
* Satisfaction from positive learning insights and motivational feedback in the app
* Learn everywhere

Value Map
---------

Products & Services
'''''''''''''''''''
  
* *Anki*: A popular flashcard program that implements spaced repetition. It brings persistency of the
  vocabulary entries, vocabulary management, synchronization, a spaced-repetition algorithm, and
  learning management out of the box.
* *Danki addon* for Anki: Learn with exercises; integrated workflow for text work and vocabulary learning with
  in-text help and auto-review; learning analysis and progress dashboard.
* *Danki app* for Android: Learn everywhere on your mobile phone
* *Danki CLI*: Build Anki decks for Danki with vocabulary, exercises and text from school books.
  
Pain Relievers
''''''''''''''

* Treat the usage of a vocabulary entry in text homework as a review of the spaced-repetition learning. 
  
  - This reduces the time spent in explicit sessions for spaced-repetition learning. Known often-used
    vocabulary entries rarely appear in spaced-repetition learning sessions. 
  - This identifies difficult vocabulary entries from text homework via help events. Spaced-repetition
    learning can focus on difficult vocabulary entries.

* Generate contextual exercises from texts for vocabulary entries the student needed help for.
* Tune spaced-repetition scheduling to the student's forgetting curve to reach a target success rate during
  vocabulary entry review.
* Connect new and difficult vocabulary entries with related knowledge to strengthen memory and keep them
  distinct from similar headwords.
* Learn with multimodal exercises (read/speak/listen/type) to memorize vocabulary entries more effectively.
* Homework-driven workflow: Prioritize homework and tests over app-driven repetition.

Gain Creators
'''''''''''''

* Ready decks for school books for easy start
* Review words that might appear in class in the next days.
* Progress metrics and recommendations
* Motivational feedback and gamification to keep the student engaged and motivated.
* Mobile app for learning
